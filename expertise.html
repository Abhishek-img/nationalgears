6-Stage Precision Gear Manufacturing Process

Every gear produced at National Gears passes through a strictly monitored multi-stage manufacturing sequence.

01

CAD/CAM & Tooth Simulation

3D modeling of gear teeth geometry, contact stress analysis, and CNC G-code optimization.

02

Material Forging & Blanking

Certified alloy steels (20MnCr5, 8620, EN24) forged and ultrasonically tested for micro-grain integrity.

03

CNC Hobbing & Shaping

High-speed tooth cutting using solid carbide hobs on multi-axis CNC gear hobbing centers.

04

Controlled Case Hardening

Atmosphere-controlled carburizing and oil quenching achieving uniform 58-62 HRC case depth.

05

Micro Flank Profile Grinding

Post-heat-treatment tooth profile grinding to achieve AGMA 12 precision and sub-micron Ra finish.

06

CMM & Lead/Pitch Inspection

Final verification on CNC Gear Inspection machines, measuring tooth lead, pitch error & runout.
